    How this number is made

    For the place and day you pick, the server asks each source below for its forecast and averages the chance of rain across whichever ones reach that day, giving the more skilful ones more weight. The bar fills to that average; the faint band behind it shows the lowest and highest chance any single source gives, so a short band means the sources agree.

    Your event's hours are gold on the hourly chart. The verdict looks only at those hours: the highest hourly chance of rain, and the average wind, humidity and temperature. Until hourly forecasts reach the date it uses the day's figures.

    Forecasts reach about 16 days ahead and are refreshed at most once an hour.

      Times are local to the event. Event Weather Tracker is open source.
