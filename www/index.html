How this number is made

Every hour this page pulls the forecast for Red Bank from each source below and averages the daily chance of rain across whichever ones cover that day. The bar fills to the average; the faint band behind it shows the lowest and highest chance any single source gives, so a short band means the sources agree.

Tap a day to see the chance hour by hour (averaged across the sources that publish hourly detail) and every source's own number.
